Two halves

Neither half is fixed.

Every hex value above is copied from scrollmark/social-skills rather than chosen here — a preset with no caption colour is an error, not a default. A preview is still a preview: it stands the colours next to each other at a card's size, where a render puts the type over footage. {{ gallery.counts.formats }} formats and {{ gallery.counts.styles }} presets ship, which is {{ gallery.counts.formats | times: gallery.counts.styles }} pairings before anyone writes a new one; a project can add its own of either, and they take precedence over the ones that ship.

The shape

A format is a scene grammar.

How many scenes, what each is for, what to ask before planning one, and what the format refuses to do. Two videos in the same format can look nothing alike. 

video-studio formats --list --aspect 9:16

The look

A style is a preset you can save.

Caption colour, outline, size and placement, plus the card roles a storyboard names. Lift one out of a video you liked, then apply it to the next. 

video-studio styles --save mine --storyboard storyboard.json
