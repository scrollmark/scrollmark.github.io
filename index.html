Social Skills

Two halves of one job. Seven skills teach Claude to read the room — tone, hooks, trend timing, and platform fluency across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Seven more make the video: ten scene grammars, footage sourced from public-domain archives or generated, narration synthesised on your own machine, a Remotion composer that renders it, and a gate that checks the result against the plan it was built from. Two more cover the method and the toolchain. Because even AI needs social skills.

Hosted MCP server

SocialGPT MCP

One URL brings your SocialGPT data into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — any agent that speaks MCP. Nothing to install, no API keys: OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, read-only by default, revoke any time.

your content competitor intel guided workflows content profile publish & post

claude mcp add --transport http socialgpt https://mcp.gpt.social/mcp

The day job

Brands on one side, creators on the other.

Scrollmark is a social intelligence platform: it turns comments, DMs and posts into structured customer data, then handles the replies and the reporting. SocialGPT is the creator-facing half — an AI copilot that studies what works on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Everything on this page fell out of that work, so it's tuned on real posts, real metrics, and real feeds.
